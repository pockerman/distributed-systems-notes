# Preface {.unnumbered}

These are my notes on designing distributed systems. They start from a single-server web application and work through the problems that appear as it grows: communication over unreliable networks, scalability and caching, replication and partitioning, databases and storage, resiliency, and security. A final part works through a couple of design exercises.

## How to use this book

- **Highlight and annotate.** Every page has a [Hypothesis](https://web.hypothes.is/) sidebar on the right.
  Select any text to highlight it or attach a note or question. Sign in to a free Hypothesis account to keep
  your annotations; use the *Only Me* group to keep them private.
- **Summaries.** Each chapter ends with a summary box of the key points.
- **References.** Each chapter lists its sources at the end. The main books referenced throughout are
  Roberto Vitillo's _Understanding Distributed Systems_, Dominik Tornow's _Think Distributed Systems_,
  Pekka Enberg's _Latency_, and Alex Xu's _System Design Interview_.
